This Privacy Policy explains how Investment Calculator (the “App”) handles information when you use it. “We”, “us”, and “our” refer to the developer identified above.

## 1. Your calculations

The App calculates investment projections on your device. It does not require an account, connect to your bank, or ask for bank account numbers, payment card details, or brokerage credentials.

The amounts, contribution settings, investment periods, return rates, and targets you enter are used locally to calculate results. The App does not intentionally transmit these values or your calculated results to us, Firebase Analytics, or advertising services. Its custom analytics events record the selected calculation mode and actions such as Calculate and Reset, rather than the financial values you enter.

Calculator state is held in memory while the App is running. Android may separately back up eligible app data according to your device settings; the App does not provide its own cloud account or calculation-sync service.

## 2. Information collected by integrated services

Although calculations happen locally, the App uses Google services that process device, usage, advertising, and diagnostic information.

### Google Analytics for Firebase

We use Firebase Analytics to understand how people use the App and which features are useful. Information may include app-instance identifiers, advertising identifiers where available and enabled, device model, operating system, language, app version, approximate geographic information, sessions, and app interactions.

The App records screen views, Calculate actions with the calculation mode, and Reset actions. Firebase also collects certain events automatically, such as first opens and app activity. These identifiers are not the same as your name, but they can distinguish an installation or device and are not necessarily anonymous.

### Firebase Crashlytics

We use Crashlytics to identify crashes and improve reliability. Reports may include installation and session identifiers, crash times, stack traces, exception messages, app version, device characteristics, operating system, and app state. Crash reports may also include automatically recorded activity breadcrumbs when Analytics is enabled.

### Google AdMob

The App displays banner and app-open advertisements through Google AdMob. Google's Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share IP addresses, advertising and other device identifiers, app and advertising interactions, and diagnostic information. An IP address may be used to estimate general location.

This information supports advertising delivery and measurement, analytics, security, and fraud prevention. Whether ads are personalized depends on applicable consent requirements, user choices, and advertising configuration. Non-personalized advertising can still involve data processing.

### Information you send us

If you contact us, we receive the email address and information you choose to include. Please avoid sending bank credentials or other sensitive financial information. We use correspondence to answer questions and handle support or privacy requests.

Calculation inputs are not stored in a developer-operated server database. Service data has separate retention periods:

* **Analytics:** 2 months (Google Analytics default event data retention period).

* **Advertising:** Google and applicable advertising partners retain data according to their policies and the purpose of processing. See the links below.
* **Support correspondence:** Retained for 1 year or until the support request is resolved.

Uninstalling the App does not automatically delete information already received by service providers.
